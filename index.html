About the Tournament

KPL Series BSU is an esports tournament organized by the Department of European Languages at the International Education and Exchange School. The competition gathers top student teams and aims to promote esports culture, teamwork, and international exchange.

Tournament Format

Group Stage → Knockout Stage → Grand Final

• Group Stage: Round Robin
• Semi Finals: BO3
• Grand Final: BO5
